# Web Management

The BIGD website is the primary digital gateway for communicating BIGD's rigorous research, policy insights, and institutional identity to global partners, policymakers, and the public. Given the volume, diversity, and impact of the institute's work, maintaining a consistent, professional, and accessible digital presence is crucial for protecting its brand integrity and maximizing the reach of its findings. This comprehensive handbook provides clear, step-by-step guidelines for the staff responsible for content submission and publication on the website.

---

## 1. General Guidelines

- **Language:** BIGD Standard British English, which is predominantly British English with the following exceptions: `programme` is spelt as `program` and words ending in `-ise`, `-yse`, are spelt with `-ize` and `-yze`.
- **Punctuation:** Use Oxford comma.
- **Case:** Use `APA Title Case` for study, publication, blog, event, and media titles.
- **Date Format:** British format `Day Month Year`.
- **Acronyms:** Spell out the full name on first mention with the acronym in parentheses; use the acronym thereafter.

## 2. Studies

### 2.1. Study Title
The official title of the study, as provided by researchers.
- **Format:** `APA Title Case` (regardless of the original case format provided).

### 2.2. Micro-Abstract
A concise summary of the study comprising the introduction, methodology, and key findings (if available).
- **Word Count:** `60-80 words`.
- **Format:** Single paragraph. *Italics*. No in-text citations.

### 2.3. Researchers
Names of the researchers involved in the study.
- **Order:** List names in the exact order suggested by the focal person.
- **Name Format:** `First Name Middle Name Last Name`.
- **Titles:** If a researcher holds a PhD, append it after the last name instead of using `Dr` at the start.
- **Separators:** Use semicolons (`;`) to separate multiple researchers. Use `and` before the very last name.
- **Hyperlinks:** Hyperlink names of internal BIGD staff to their respective BIGD profile pages.

### 2.4. Timeline
Reflects the project duration.
- **Same Year:** `Month-Month Year` (e.g., April–November 2025).
- **Multi-Year:** `Year-Year` (e.g., 2023–2025).
- **Note:** Use an en-dash (`–`) for ranges, not a hyphen (`-`).

### 2.5. Status
Reflects the current phase.
- **Ongoing:** Current date is between the start and end dates.
- **Completed:** Current date is past the end date.

### 2.6. Contact
The specific individual handling inquiries for this study.
- **Format:** `First Name Middle Name Last Name, Title; email@bigd.ac.bd`

### 2.7. Context
Background information setting the stage for why the research was conducted.
- **Word Count:** `100-120 words`.
- **Format:** Single paragraph. Do not use in-text citations.

### 2.8. Objectives
Specific research goals.
- **Word Count:** `80-100 words`.
- **Format:** Single paragraph and/or bullet points. Do not use numbered or alphabetized lists. If the study uses research questions, rephrase them into objectives. Do not use in-text citations.

### 2.9. Methodology
A high-level overview of the approach followed by the study. Avoid excessive technical jargon.
- **Word Count:** `120-150 words`.
- **Format:** Single paragraph. Do not use in-text citations.

### 2.10. Findings and Recommendations
Key results and recommendations. This section is more substantial than previous sections.
- **Word Count:** `180-250 words`.
- **Format:** Multiple paragraphs are permitted. Do not use in-text citations.
- **Ongoing Studies:** If data is unavailable, explicitly state: `"Findings are forthcoming."`

### 2.11. Topics
Select one or more relevant tags from the approved list:
`BRAC Programs`, `Cities`, `Climate Change`, `COVID-19`, `Datahub`, `Digitization`, `Economic Development`, `Education`, `Gender and Social Development`, `Governance and Politics`, `Humanitarian`, `Macroeconomics`, `Monitoring and Evaluation`, `Skills and Jobs`, `Social Transformation`, `Ultra-Poor Graduation`, `WEE-Connect`, `WEE-DiFine`.

### 2.12. Related Publications
A list of outputs (journal articles, reports, working papers, etc.) produced under this study is dynamically populated.
- **Order:** Chronological (`latest on top`).

## 3. Publications

### 3.1. Title
The official title of the publication, as written in the source document.
- **Format:** `APA Title Case` (regardless of original case format).

### 3.2. Abstract
A concise summary of the content.

**Scenario A: Original Abstract Available**
Use the original abstract.
- **Modifications:** Convert structured abstracts into a single unstructured paragraph. Adjust spelling, capitalization, and punctuation to match the `BIGD Style Manual`.

**Scenario B: No Abstract Available**
Write a suitable abstract including introduction, methodology, and findings.
- **Word Count:** `120-250 words`.
- **Format:** Single paragraph. Do not use in-text citations.

### 3.3. Authors
Names of the authors of the publication, as listed on the source material.
- **Order:** List names in the exact order as listed in the publication.
- **Name Format:** `Last Name, First Name Middle Name`.
- **Titles:** Do not use titles (e.g., `Dr.`, `PhD`, `Prof.`).
- **Separators:** Use semicolons (`;`) to separate multiple authors. Use `and` before the last author.

### 3.4. Publisher
Name of the publisher.
- **Internal:** If BIGD is the publisher, write `BIGD`.
- **External:** Provide the full official title of the publisher (include acronym in parentheses if common).
- **Multiple:** Separate with `and` + Oxford comma.

### 3.5. Year
The year the publication was published.
- **Format:** `4-digit year` (e.g., 2024).

### 3.6. Publication Type
Select the single most relevant type:
`Journal Article`, `Working Paper`, `Book/Book Chapter`, `Monograph`, `Report`, `Conference Proceeding`, `White Paper`, `Review/Synthesis Paper`, `Evidence and Insights`, `Policy Brief`, `BIGD Review`, `Case Study`, `Position Paper`, `Data`, `Multimedia`.

### 3.7. Topics
Select one or more relevant tags from the approved list:
`BRAC Programs`, `Cities`, `Climate Change`, `COVID-19`, `Datahub`, `Digitization`, `Economic Development`, `Education`, `Gender and Social Development`, `Governance and Politics`, `Humanitarian`, `Macroeconomics`, `Monitoring and Evaluation`, `Skills and Jobs`, `Social Transformation`, `Ultra-Poor Graduation`, `WEE-Connect`, `WEE-DiFine`.

### 3.8. Access/Download
- **Primary:** Provide the `DOI Link`.
- **Secondary:** If no DOI is available, provide the direct link to the publication.
- **Paywall Rule:** If the item is paywalled, write a short blog post (approximately `500 words`) summarizing the paper. Place the link to this blog at the bottom.

## 4. Blogs

### 4.1. Title
Title of the blog post, as suggested by the author.
- **Format:** `APA Title Case` (regardless of the provided case format).

### 4.2. Date
The date of the blog publication.
- **Format:** `Day Month Year` (e.g., 12 December 2025).

### 4.3. Authors
Names of the authors of the blog post.
- **Name Format:** `First Name Middle Name Last Name`.
- **Titles:** Append titles after the name (e.g., `Name, PhD`).
- **Separators:** Separate multiple authors with commas, with `and` before the last author.

### 4.4. Main Body
- **Style:** Spelling, capitalization, and punctuation must be consistent with the `BIGD Style Manual`, regardless of the original content provided.
- **Headings:** Retain up to two levels of headings if used by authors.
  - Level 1: `Bold`, `Title Case`, `20px` (1.25 rem) font.
  - Level 2: `Bold`, `Title Case`, `Italics`, `16px` (1 rem) font.
- **Citations:** Do not use in-text citations or footnotes. Use hyperlinks instead.
- **Endnotes:** Use numerals (e.g., `[1]`, `[2]`) only for non-linkable clarifications. List these at the bottom under a horizontal line (no heading).

### 4.5. Visuals (Images/Tables/Figures)
- **Captions:** `Sentence case`. End with a full stop.
- **Credits:** Credit the source if necessary.
- **Numbering:** Do not number figures or tables.

### 4.6. Blog Snippets (For Listing Pages)
- **Thumbnail:** High-quality image representing the theme.
- **Short Teaser:** A brief, compelling hook that expands on the title and entices the reader to click (`15-30 words`).

## 5. Events

### 5.1. Title
Official title of the event, as suggested by the focal person.
- **Format:** `APA Title Case` (regardless of the case format provided).

### 5.2. Logistics
- **Date:** `Day Month Year` (e.g., 12 December 2025).
- **Time:** Include `AM/PM` and specify the `Time Zone`.
- **Location:** Venue name. If held virtually, state `"Online"`.

### 5.3. Event Description
- **Banner:** Provide event banner if available.

**Scenario A: Upcoming Events**
- **Word Count:** `150-250 words`.
- **Tense:** Future tense.
- **Content:** Context, organizers, agenda, key participants, and a call to action (e.g., registration link).

**Scenario B: Past Events**
- **Word Count:** `200-300 words`.
- **Tense:** Past tense.
- **Content:** Context, organizers, agenda, key participants, key discussions, and notable quotes from high-profile attendees.
- **Visuals:** Include selected key images at the bottom with appropriate captions.

Title of the media content, as written in the source material.
- **Format:** `APA Title Case` (regardless of the original case format).

### 6.2. Publisher/Source

- **Word Count:** `60-100 words`.
- **Format:** Single paragraph.

### 6.5. Visuals
The official logo of the publisher or an image representative of the announcement.
- **News & Op-eds:** Upload the official logo of the publisher/media outlet.
- **Announcements:** Upload the official banner or a suitable, high-quality photo from the event.

### 6.6. Topics

## 7. People

### 7.1. Headshot
High-quality, professional headshot of the individual.
- **Specs:** A square headshot with the face clearly visible.
- **Recency:** Photo should be within the last `2-3 years`.

### 7.2. Name and Title
Full name and BIGD designation of the person.
- **Name Format:** `First Name Middle Name Last Name`.
  - *PhD Holders:* Append `, PhD` after the last name.
- **Designation:** Official job title at BIGD.
  - *Note:* Do not abbreviate `Senior` to `Sr.`

### 7.3. Contact
Official BIGD email address.
- **Format:** Use `lowercase`.

### 7.4. Bio
A concise yet comprehensive overview of the individual.
- **Word Count:** `150-300 words`.
- **Content:** Role at BIGD, expertise, research interests (for academics), previous work experience, honours/recognitions, and academic qualifications.
- **Exclusions:** Do not include personal hobbies, unrelated interests, excessive CV details, or first-person narrative (`"I am..."`).

### 7.5. Links & Resources
- **External:** Google Scholar profile link (for academics only).
- **CV:** Link to the `PDF` of the CV uploaded to the BIGD website (for academics only).
- **Associated Content:** Automatically generated list of publications, op-eds, blogs, studies, and projects (sorted newest first).
